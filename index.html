Chip
Flipper

An idle tycoon about the GPU market

Coming to Google Play. Join the beta

You buy graphics cards at launch and relist them by lunch. Then you buy a sand pit. Then a cleanroom. Eventually you are building chips for an AI that nobody asked for, in a factory with its own weather.

Every factory is a chain: sand, lithography, shipping, and later sales. The slowest stage sets the pace, and a marker shows you which one it is. Buy there. Then it moves somewhere else.

Three eras, a tech tree full of card models, a market that follows the headlines, and a factory that keeps running while the game is closed.
